# ADR-048: SMS Uses SwiftSMS With BeamSMS Failover (Supersedes ADR-033)

- **Status:** Active (Supersedes ADR-033)
- **Date:** 2026-10-09
- **Deciders:** David Kim, Sarah Chen, Meera Patel
- **Decision ID:** DEC-032
- **Ticket Ref:** LIN-415
- **Supersedes:** DEC-028 (ADR-033)

## Context
Incident INC-79 on 2026-10-05: a two-hour SwiftSMS regional outage failed 41 percent of checkout OTP texts. ADR-033 had accepted a single provider for the volume discount.

## Decision
The notification dispatcher sends through SwiftSMS first and fails over to BeamSMS after two consecutive errors. A health probe re-enables the primary when it recovers (PR-1198).

## Consequences
- OTP delivery survives the loss of one carrier.
- Two vendor contracts and two delivery-report formats to maintain.
